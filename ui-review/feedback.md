# UI feedback

Write feedback here any time, even while a loop is running. It's picked up at the start of each round.
One bullet per item. Optional tags: `[must]` do this round · `[idea]` if it fits · `[no] …` veto (don't do it, stop suggesting it) · `[question]` just answer it.

Examples:
- [must] The "Needs attention" badge is too alarming for someone who starts in 4 days
- [no] dark mode
- [question] Would a progress ring read better than the bar?

## Inbox

## Standing preferences
Rules that apply every round. Add "always…/never…" items here.

- Keep it a plain HTML/CSS/JS app with no build step.
- The product is branded **MangoApps** (not BrightPath).
- Keep the blue brand colour and the overall calm, simple look.
- The new hire's home screen shows at most 3 focus tasks.
- Wording is neutral, supportive and action-oriented, never punishing: no "owe", "behind", "overdue", "late", "failed" or "missed" in visible copy. Say what's next ("1 for you", "3 to catch up", "was due Nov 4").
- Charts stay simple: one question per chart, answered in plain words; at most 2 colours; no legends, hatching or per-item marks; one number, not three.
